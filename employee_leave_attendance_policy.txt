NORTHSTAR WORKS — EMPLOYEE LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE POLICY
Fictional training document | Version 1.0 | Effective 1 October 2026
Document owner: People Operations | Policy ID: HR-001

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy applies to all full-time employees of fictional Northstar Works. Contractors follow their individual agreements. This document is a training example, not a real employer policy.

2. WORKING HOURS
The standard workweek is Monday through Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, including a 45-minute lunch break. Employees should notify their manager if they expect to arrive more than 15 minutes late. Flexible start times require manager approval.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 18 paid annual-leave days per calendar year. Employees joining midyear receive a prorated allowance. Requests for three or more consecutive days should be submitted at least 10 working days in advance through the HR portal. Managers normally respond within three working days. Up to five unused annual-leave days can be carried into the following calendar year; carried days expire on 31 March.

4. SICK LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick-leave days per calendar year. Employees should notify their manager and People Operations as early as possible on the first day of absence. For absences longer than two consecutive working days, a medical certificate may be requested. Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

5. PERSONAL AND EMERGENCY LEAVE
Employees may take up to three paid personal/emergency leave days each calendar year. Notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible. These days cannot be carried forward.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Northstar Works publishes the applicable holiday calendar each December for the next year. Public holidays are separate from annual leave.

7. REMOTE WORK AND ATTENDANCE
Employees may request up to two remote-work days per week with manager approval. Remote employees must be reachable during agreed core hours, 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Attendance is recorded in the HR portal by the end of each workday.

8. LEAVE APPROVAL AND ESCALATION
Managers approve or reject leave requests based on staffing needs and policy. If a request remains unanswered after three working days, contact People Operations at peopleops@northstar.example. Employees should not assume leave is approved until confirmation appears in the HR portal.

9. EXCEPTIONS
Any exception to this policy requires written approval from the Head of People Operations. The policy does not define maternity, parental, or bereavement leave; employees should contact People Operations for those topics.

10. DOCUMENT CONTROL
Owner: People Operations. Review cycle: annually. Next review: October 2027.
